# Ascendant: Mythic Rebuilt

*A Mythic Alternative for Pathfinder Second Edition*

> There is an arrangement by which the world limits what a person may become. You are no longer party to it.

---

## Using These Rules

These rules replace the Mythic rules entirely. Do not use both. Where Mythic grants destinies, calling, and mythic points, Ascendant grants Talents that scale across all twenty levels and require no separate resource to spend.

Characters built with these rules are dramatically more powerful than standard Pathfinder Second Edition assumes. Ability scores climb higher and faster, proficiency arrives early and reaches beyond Legendary, and every character chooses three permanent Talents that define what they are.

This is a deliberate escalation, not a balanced sidegrade. Player characters will land hits, resist effects, and survive punishment well beyond what the Bestiary expects. The ruleset assumes the Game Master will push back with tougher opposition. Every bonus here is a flat numerical increase or a bounded effect, so monster-side adjustments remain fully effective.

### Character Creation

- Apply Enhanced Attribute Boosts.
- Choose an Expertise Track.
- Choose one Apex Talent, one Paragon Talent, and one Adept Talent.
- The party agrees together on a single Party Talent.

*All of these choices are made at 1st level and are permanent.*

### Assumed Variant Rules

Ascendant assumes three of Pathfinder's optional variant rules are in play, all of them from 1st level. The Talents and the Expertise Tracks are balanced against a character who already has them.

- **Dual Class.** Every character has two classes, gaining the class features, class feats, and proficiencies of both, taking the better value wherever the two overlap.
- **Free Archetype.** Every character gains an additional class feat at every even level, usable only for archetype feats.
- **Ancestry Paragon.** Every character gains an ancestry feat at 1st level and at every odd level thereafter.

*Where a Talent refers to your proficiency rank being one rank higher than normal, normal means the better of your two classes.*

## Enhanced Attribute Boosts

Characters gain their normal ability boosts from ancestry, background, class, and free boosts exactly as described in the Core Rulebook. In addition, they gain the following.

### Initial Boosts

- At 1st level you gain four additional ability boosts.
  - These follow the normal rules for ability boosts. You cannot apply more than one to the same ability score, and each increases a score by 2, or by 1 if the score is already 18 or higher.

### Continuing Boosts

- At 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th level you gain two additional ability boosts.
  - These follow no restrictions. Both may apply to the same score, and each always increases it by exactly 2 regardless of its current value.

### Attribute Caps

No ability score may exceed the following values. This cap applies to all boosts.

| Level | Cap | Modifier |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 20 | +5 |
| 5th | 22 | +6 |
| 10th | 24 | +7 |
| 15th | 26 | +8 |
| 20th | 28 | +9 |

*Standard Pathfinder stops a 1st-level score at 18. A score at 18 still gains only +1 from each boost, but Dual Class adds a second class boost, so together with an initial boost a character may begin play at 20 (+5).*

## Advanced Proficiency

At 1st level, choose one of the two Expertise Tracks below. This choice is permanent.

### Weapon Expertise

Applies to all weapon and unarmed attacks you are trained in, regardless of group or category.

**From 1st Level**
- Your proficiency rank is one rank higher than your classes would normally grant, up to Mythic.
- Add a damage bonus to your Strikes equal to your proficiency rank.

**Minimum Rank**
- Your proficiency rank is at least Master at 5th level, Legendary at 10th, and Mythic at 15th.

**Classes That Begin at Expert**
- If a class makes you Expert with a weapon at 1st level, as the Fighter and Gunslinger do, you stay a rank ahead of the minimum with it: Master at 1st, Legendary at 5th, and Mythic at 13th.
- At 15th level, your Strikes with those weapons gain one additional weapon damage die. This stacks with striking runes.

### Spellcasting Expertise

Applies to spell attack rolls and spell DCs for all spells you can cast, from any tradition or source, including innate spells, focus spells, and spells cast from items.

**1st Level**
- Your proficiency rank is Expert.
- Add a damage bonus to each creature damaged by your spells equal to your proficiency rank.
- Add your spellcasting attribute modifier to the damage your spells deal to each creature.
  - If a spell damages the same creature in multiple instances, total that damage first, then add the modifier once.
  - For basic saving throws, both bonuses count as part of the spell's basic damage.

**5th Level**
- Your proficiency rank becomes Master.

**10th Level**
- Your proficiency rank becomes Legendary.

**15th Level**
- Your proficiency rank becomes Mythic.

### Enhanced Defense

Both tracks grant the following.

- Your Armor Class increases by 1 at 1st level, 2 at 5th, 3 at 10th, and 4 at 15th.
  - This is not a bonus and stacks with all bonuses to Armor Class.

## Mythic Proficiency

Mythic is a new proficiency rank found only through this ruleset, granting a proficiency bonus equal to your level + 10.

| Rank | Bonus | Rider |
|---|---|---|
| Trained | Level + 2 | +2 |
| Expert | Level + 4 | +4 |
| Master | Level + 6 | +6 |
| Legendary | Level + 8 | +8 |
| Mythic | Level + 10 | +10 |

## Expanded Investment

- The normal limit of 10 invested magic items increases to 20.

# Apex Talents

*Choose one at 1st level. Apex Talents are the most powerful options in this ruleset and develop through 20th level. Your Apex Talent is the single thing that most defines what your character is.*

## Bonded Soul

> *Somewhere in the working that shaped you, a second thing arrived and declined to leave. It wears whatever form the bond permits and shares every wound you take — which makes the arrangement less a partnership than a single life, awkwardly divided between two bodies.*

**Statistics**
- The Bonded is your level. It uses your AC, saving throw modifiers, Perception, and class DC.
- Its Strikes use the higher of your weapon attack modifier or your spell attack modifier, and add your highest attribute modifier to damage.
- It has its own multiple attack penalty.
- It uses your skill modifiers. Its Athletics modifier is the higher of your Athletics or your best attack modifier.

**Shared Life**
- You and the Bonded share a single Hit Point pool. Damage to either reduces it; healing either restores it.
  - If both are affected by the same area effect, apply the greater result once.
- You share conditions, taking the worse of the two.

**Manifesting**
- Manifest it in an open space within 30 feet as a 2-action activity. It remains indefinitely and may travel any distance from you.
- You may dismiss it as a free action, and it may dismiss itself as a free action.
- If your shared Hit Points drop to one quarter of maximum or lower, it immediately unmanifests.

**Actions and Damage**
- It acts on your initiative, immediately after your turn, with 2 actions. At 10th level it gains a third action.
- Its Strikes gain an additional damage die at 5th, 10th, and 15th level.

**Evolution Pool**
- You have evolution points equal to 3 + your level, reallocated freely during daily preparations.

### Base Form

Choose one at 1st level, permanently.

| Form | Speed | Strike | Granted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elemental | 30 ft | slam 1d8 B | Strikes deal +1d6 of a chosen elemental type (acid, cold, electricity, fire, sonic). Immune to that type. At 10th the rider becomes 2d6. |
| Fiend | 25 ft | claws 1d6 S (agile) | Darkvision and the unholy trait. Resistance to fire 5, 10 at 10th. At 5th its Strikes gain the unholy trait and deal +1d6 spirit to holy creatures. |
| Celestial | 25 ft, fly 20 ft | slam 1d8 B | The holy trait. At 5th, once per encounter it may restore Hit Points equal to your level to an adjacent creature as an action, and its Strikes deal +1d6 spirit to unholy creatures. |

| Aberration | 25 ft, climb 25 ft | tentacle 1d6 B (reach 10 ft) | Climb. Tentacle Strikes have the grapple trait. At 5th, creatures it has Grabbed take mental damage equal to half your level at the start of their turn. |
| Undead | 25 ft | claws 1d6 S (agile) | Void healing: healed by void, harmed by vitality. Immune to disease and poison. Doesn't breathe. |

### Evolutions

**1 Point**
- **Extra Strike** — Gain one additional unarmed Strike from the list below, dealing damage equal to your base Strike's die. Maximum 2.
- **Improved Damage** — One Strike's damage die increases one step, to a maximum of d12. Maximum 2, each on a different Strike.
- **Climb / Swim** — Gain the chosen Speed, equal to its land Speed.
- **Swift** — +10 feet to all Speeds. Maximum 2.
- **Keen Senses** — Darkvision and imprecise scent to 60 feet.

|---|---|---|

| Gore | piercing | deadly d8 |
| Slam | bludgeoning | shove |

**2 Points**
- **Limbs** — A pair of arms with hands. It can wield weapons and Interact with objects.
- **Burrow** — Burrow Speed equal to half its land Speed, leaving no tunnel.
- **Energy Attacks** — Strikes deal additional damage of a chosen type equal to its base Strike's damage die.
- **Large** — Becomes Large, gaining reach 10 feet and +2 to Athletics.
- **Ironform** — +1 to its AC. Maximum 3.
- **Grab** — Choose a Strike. It gains the grapple trait, and on a hit the Bonded may attempt to Grapple as a free action.
- **Knockdown** — Choose a Strike. On a hit the Bonded may attempt to Trip as a free action.

**3 Points**
- **Flight** — Fly Speed equal to its land Speed.
- **Immunity** — Immune to one damage type or one condition.
- **Regeneration** — Regeneration equal to half your level, deactivated by the damage type matching your base form: spirit for Fiend and Celestial, vitality for Undead, void for Beast, mental for Aberration, or its chosen elemental type for Elemental.
- **Dread Form** — Enemies that begin their turn within 30 feet are Frightened 1 unless they succeed at a Will save against your class DC.

**4 Points**
- **Huge** — Becomes Huge, gaining reach 15 feet and +4 to Athletics. Requires Large.
- **Warded** — When targeted by a spell, it may attempt to counteract that spell as a reaction, using your class DC and half your level as the counteract rank.

## Borrowed Time

> *Time moves around you rather than through you. Where others must choose between the strike and the step, you take both and find a moment left over — and no one, least of all you, can say where that moment came from.*

**1st Level**
- You are Quickened. Use the extra action however you like.
- Immune to Slowed, which does not negate your Quickened condition, and immune to Immobilized.
- +20 feet to all Speeds.
- When you Hustle, multiply your Speed by 10.
- Your movement does not trigger reactions when you Stride, Climb, Crawl, Fly, or Swim.

**3rd Level and Every 3 Levels**
- Gain an extra reaction at the start of your turn: 2 at 3rd, 3 at 6th, 4 at 9th, 5 at 12th, 6 at 15th, 7 at 18th.

**5th / 10th / 15th / 20th Level**
- Speed bonus increases to +30, +40, +50, then +60 feet.

**7th Level**
- One extra flourish action per round, paying its normal action cost.

**13th Level**
- Any number of flourish actions per round.

## Charmed Life

> *The odds do not refuse you so much as politely decline to be involved. Blades turn at the last instant, footing holds where it has no business holding, and those who travel with you learn not to comment on it, in case the comment is what breaks the spell.*

**1st Level**
- Your maximum Hero Points increase to 6, and you begin each session with 4.
- Whenever you roll a natural 1 on any die, reroll it and take the new result.
  - If you roll another 1, continue rerolling until you do not.
- Immune to misfortune effects. When targeted by one, it instead rebounds on its source.

**5th Level**
- When you fail or critically fail a saving throw, roll a DC 11 flat check. On a success, treat the save as one degree better.
  - As with any check, you may spend a Hero Point to reroll this flat check.

**10th Level**
- The flat check DC becomes 6.

**15th Level**
- You automatically succeed at all flat checks, including this one.
- Your failed and critically failed saves always improve by one degree.

**20th Level**
- This improvement applies to any saving throw result, including successes.

*Resolve this talent's improvement before applying class features that further adjust your degree of success, such as Evasion, Juggernaut, or Resolve.*

## Deathless

> *Whatever governs the closing of wounds has decided, in your case, to be generous. Bones knit while the blow that broke them is still landing, and the slow indignities that wear other bodies down — plague, venom, exhaustion, age itself — find nothing in you willing to yield.*

**1st Level**
- Regeneration equal to twice your level.
  - Choose spirit, vitality, or void at character creation. That damage type deactivates it.
- Immune to all persistent damage, poison, and disease.
- You do not need to eat, drink, or sleep.
- Conditions that would reduce by 1 at the end of your turn are removed instead.
- Choose two damage types. You gain resistance 5 to each, changeable during daily preparations.

**5th Level**
- Chosen resistances increase to 10.

**7th Level**
- Immune to Drained.
- Regeneration increases to three times your level.
- Choose one resistant type and become immune to it.

**9th Level**
- Chosen resistances increase to 15.

**11th Level**
- Immune to Clumsy and Enfeebled.

**13th Level**
- Gain Resist All 5.

**15th Level**
- You no longer need to breathe.
- Choose a second type to become immune to.

**17th Level**
- Chosen resistances increase to 20.
- Resist All increases to 10.

**19th Level**
- You no longer gain the Unconscious condition at 0 Hit Points unless your regeneration is deactivated. You are instead Slowed 1 until you begin a turn above 0 Hit Points.

*Your regeneration is deactivated when you take at least 1 point of damage of your chosen type, or from an effect bearing a matching trait. Damage absorbed by temporary Hit Points counts. A missed attack, or a save that reduces the damage to 0, does not. You cannot select your deactivating type as one of your chosen resistances or immunities, and no spell, item, or other effect can grant you resistance or immunity to it. Resist All gained from this talent is the sole exception, and applies to your deactivating type normally.*

## Dread Presence

> *Something in you registers below thought, in the part of the mind that predates language and still knows what it is to be prey. Brave creatures find their hands unsteady near you, and the mindless, who should feel nothing at all, discover they would rather be elsewhere.*

**1st Level**
- As a free action once per round, activate or deactivate an aura with a radius of 10 feet + 5 feet per even level.
- When an enemy first enters the aura, or your movement first places them inside, they attempt a Will save against the higher of your Class DC or Spell DC.
  - **Critical Success** Frightened 1. **Success** Frightened 2. **Failure** Frightened 3.
  - **Critical Failure** Frightened 4. The creature drops anything it is holding and cannot use reactions while in your aura.
  - While inside the aura, and for 1 round after leaving, they cannot reduce their Frightened value below the result rolled. On a critical failure this lasts 1 minute.
  - An enemy that leaves and re-enters rerolls, taking the worse result.
- Creatures immune to fear or emotion effects are not immune to your aura, but treat their save result as one degree better.
  - At 10th level they roll normally, and creatures that are mindless or immune to mental effects become affected, treating their result as one degree better.
  - At 17th level, nothing is immune to your aura and all creatures roll normally.

**3rd Level**
- Creatures affected by your aura are Off-Guard to you.
- When a creature Frightened by your aura is reduced to 0 Hit Points by any source, you restore Hit Points equal to half your level. This triggers no more than once per round.

**5th Level**
- A creature Frightened by your aura that rolls at or below its Frightened value on a save against any of your effects treats the result as one degree worse.

**7th Level**
- Enemies cannot reduce their Frightened value below 2 while in your aura.

**10th Level**
- The healing from your aura increases to your level, and may trigger twice per round.

**13th Level**
- Enemies cannot reduce their Frightened value below 3 while in your aura.

**15th Level**
- Enemies in your aura take an untyped penalty to attack rolls against you equal to their Frightened value. This is deliberately untyped so that it stacks with the penalty from the Frightened condition itself.

**20th Level**
- Enemies cannot willingly move closer to you while inside your aura. They may still attack, cast, and move within or out of it.

*Dropped items may be retrieved normally with an Interact action.*

## Elemental Embodiment

> *The distinction between the one who wields and the thing wielded has quietly collapsed. You do not summon your element or command it; you are what it does when it decides to take a shape and walk around.*

**1st Level**
- Choose one element permanently. You are immune to your element's damage type.
- All damage you deal may be converted to your element as a free action.
- Your unarmed Strikes deal elemental damage equal to their own damage die, or 1d6 if that die is smaller, and gain the reach trait.
- Your weapon Strikes deal additional damage of your element equal to the weapon's damage die.
- You gain your element's property.

**5th Level**
- You no longer need to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep, and are unaffected by environmental conditions related to your element.
- Creatures that touch you, or hit you with an unarmed or melee attack, take damage equal to half your level.

**10th Level**
- Your element's damage ignores resistance.
- Creatures within 10 feet take damage equal to half your level when they end their turn there.

**13th Level**
- Creatures damaged by your element suffer your element's rider.

**15th Level**
- You become your element. You are immune to precision damage and critical hits, and cannot be Grabbed or Restrained.
- You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed. The manner of this flight suits your element: borne on flame, carried on wind, riding a wave, floating on a plinth of stone.
- You may pass through any gap your element could.

**20th Level**
- Your element's damage ignores immunity, dealing half damage instead of none.
- The damaging area around you extends to 20 feet.

| Element | Damage | Property | Rider (13th) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | fire | — | Persistent fire damage equal to your level |
| Water | cold | — | Slowed 1 until the end of their next turn |
| Air | electricity | — | Damage chains to one other creature within 15 feet |
| Earth | bludgeoning | You cannot be moved, pushed, pulled, or knocked Prone against your will | Speeds reduced by 10 feet and cannot Fly or Leap, until the end of your next turn |
| Metal | slashing or piercing, chosen at character creation | Counts as cold iron, dawnsilver, or adamantine, your choice | Resistances and hardness halved until the end of your next turn |
| Wood | bludgeoning | Strikes gain grapple and trip; on a hit you may Grapple or Trip as a free action | Immobilized until they Escape, and you restore Hit Points equal to the damage dealt, once per turn |

## Lifeline

> *You have tied your life to theirs with something that cannot be cut from the outside. Their wounds arrive in your body, their afflictions find your veins, and every protection you raise for yourself settles over all of them at once — which works splendidly until you fall.*

**1st Level**
- You gain the Vital Bond focus spell, and add 1 to your maximum focus pool as normal.
- When you restore Hit Points, add twice your spell proficiency bonus to each target: +4 Trained, +8 Expert, +12 Master, +16 Legendary, +20 Mythic.
- You may restore Hit Points at a range of 60 feet regardless of a spell's normal range.

**Vital Bond** — [1 action] (concentrate, focus) — **Range** 60 feet; **Targets** any number of allies; **Duration** until your next daily preparations.
- While a target is bonded, whenever it would take damage you may take any amount of that damage yourself instead. This requires no action and has no frequency limit.
- Damage taken this way applies your own immunities, resistances, and weaknesses rather than the target's.
- The bond is suspended while a target is further than 60 feet from you, and ends entirely if you fall unconscious.

**5th Level**
- Bonded allies gain your resistances and immunities while within the bond's range.

**10th Level**
- The bond's range increases to 120 feet.
- When you Cast a Spell that targets a single creature and does not restore Hit Points, deal damage, or impose a condition, every bonded ally gains its effects as well.
- A 2-action spell restoring Hit Points to a single bonded ally costs 1 action instead and ignores the spell's normal range.

**15th Level**
- When a bonded ally would gain a condition, you may gain it yourself instead.
- Bonded allies cannot be reduced below 1 Hit Point while you are conscious and above 0 Hit Points.

**20th Level**
- Once per day, as a free action, restore every ally within 60 feet to full Hit Points and remove all conditions from them.

## Soulbound Arsenal

> *The weapon is not carried so much as recalled. It answers to a shape you hold in mind rather than a shape it was forged into, it cannot be taken from you by any means, and on the days you are too broken to lift it, it has been known to fight on without you.*

**1st Level**
- Gain a bonded weapon. As a free action once per round it takes the form of any simple, martial, or advanced melee or ranged weapon.
  - Ranged forms generate their own ammunition and never need reloading.
- It cannot be disarmed, stolen, or left behind, and returns to your hand at the start of your turn.
- It is a +1 striking weapon.
- Your proficiency rank with it equals your highest weapon or unarmed proficiency rank, regardless of its current form.

**3rd Level**
- Whenever you change its form, choose one precious material. It counts as that material for overcoming resistances, weaknesses, and immunities.
  - It gains none of that material's other properties, and its grade is the highest available at your level.
- The weapon gains its own reaction each round, separate from yours, usable only for Bonded Strike.
  - **Bonded Strike** [reaction] — **Trigger** An enemy moves within the weapon's reach. **Effect** The weapon Strikes that enemy. This Strike neither counts toward nor against your multiattack penalty.

**5th Level**
- +1 striking with one property rune.
- Choose a weapon group. You gain its critical specialization effect with this weapon in any form, and may change the group whenever you change its form.

**7th Level**
- +2 striking, two property runes.

**9th Level**
- +2 greater striking, two property runes.
- The weapon acts independently using your statistics, and may use Bonded Strike even while you are Grabbed, Restrained, Immobilized, or unconscious.

**11th Level**
- You may change the weapon's form twice per round.

**13th Level**
- +3 greater striking, three property runes.

**15th Level**
- +3 major striking. Gains the deadly d12 trait.
- The weapon gains a second reaction each round, usable only for Bonded Strike.

**17th Level**
- +4 major striking, four property runes.

**19th Level**
- You may change the weapon's form any number of times per round.

*Property runes are determined whenever you change the weapon's form and must be of a level equal to or less than your level + 2.*

## Unbound Casting

> *Magic in other hands is a negotiation: the right words, the right gestures, the right undisturbed moment. You have dispensed with the formalities. Bound, silenced, mid-fall, or barely conscious, the working arrives exactly as intended and reshapes itself on the way out.*

**1st Level**
- Your spells lose the manipulate trait.
- You gain Empowered Casting and one other Unbound Spellshape of your choice.
- Once per round, you may use a spellshape as a free action.

**3rd Level**
- Your spells lose the concentrate trait.
- Learn another spellshape.

**7th Level**
- All your spells are considered subtle.
- Learn another spellshape.

**10th Level**
- You may Cast a Spell even while Paralyzed, Stunned, Slowed, or otherwise unable to act, though you must still be conscious.
- Learn another spellshape.

**13th Level**
- You may apply two spellshapes to the same spell, rather than one. This limit does not increase.
- Learn another spellshape.

**15th Level**
- Your spells remove the incapacitation trait.

**20th Level**
- Your spells cannot be dispelled.
- You may use spellshapes as free actions with no frequency limit, except Perfect Quickening, which remains once per turn.

### Unbound Spellshapes

*These require no additional components, gestures, or words. They have no traits and cannot be identified, counteracted, or prevented.*

- **Empowered Casting** [free action] — Increase the damage dice of your next spell by half, rounded down.
- **Split Casting** [free action] — Your next spell that targets one creature targets two instead, at full effect against each.
- **Bouncing Casting** [free action] — If your next spell has no effect on its target, through a successful save, a miss, an immunity, or a counteract, it immediately retargets a different creature within range of your choice. It may bounce once.
  - A spell bounces only if the target takes no damage and suffers no condition.
- **Delayed Casting** [free action] — Your next spell doesn't take effect until the start of your next turn. You choose its targets and areas when it resolves.
- **Anchored Casting** [free action] — Your next spell with a duration of 1 minute or less doesn't require Sustain and lasts its full duration.
- **Piercing Casting** [free action] — Your next spell ignores cover and concealment and requires no line of effect, only line of sight or knowledge of the target's location.
- **Perfect Reach** [free action] — Your next spell's range becomes 500 feet, or it gains a range of 500 feet if it normally has a range of touch.
- **Perfect Quickening** [free action, once per turn] — Your next spell costs 1 fewer action, to a minimum of 1.

## Unbroken Tempo

> *There is a rhythm to violence, and it is built around the assumption that a fighter must recover between blows. You have never needed the recovery, so the second strike lands as true as the first, and the third as true as the second.*

**1st Level**
- Your multiattack penalty is -4, or -3 with agile weapons.

**3rd Level**
- Your multiattack penalty is -3, or -2 with agile weapons.

**7th Level**
- Your multiattack penalty is -2, or -1 with agile weapons.

**12th Level**
- You are no longer subject to multiattack penalties.

**15th Level**
- Conditions that reduce the number of actions you gain at the start of your turn have no effect on you.

**20th Level**
- Once per round, when you critically hit with a Strike, you may immediately make another Strike against the same target with the same weapon as a free action.

## Warmage

> *You were never able to see why the choice was necessary. The blade goes where the spell goes, arrives in the same instant, and draws on the same trained certainty — and the enemy who prepared for one of them prepared for half a problem.*

**1st Level**
- For all Strikes, spell attack rolls, and spell DCs, use the higher of your weapon statistics or your spell statistics, covering both proficiency rank and attribute modifier.
- Gain Spellstrike.
  - **Spellstrike** [2 actions] — Make a melee Strike and Cast a Spell as part of the same activity. The spell may be any spell you can cast with a casting time of 3 actions or fewer, targeting whatever it normally targets.
  - If the spell requires a spell attack roll against the creature you Struck, use the Strike's attack roll for both.

**5th Level**
- Spellstrike costs 1 action.

**10th Level**
- On a critical hit with the Strike, a spell delivered against that target is also critical.
- Spellstrike no longer counts against your multiattack penalty.

**15th Level**
- Gain Reactive Spellstrike.
  - **Reactive Spellstrike** [reaction] — **Trigger** A creature within your reach uses an action that would trigger a Reactive Strike, or moves within your reach. **Effect** You Spellstrike that creature.

# Game Master Notes

## What the Numbers Say

Every figure in this section was computed against the creature-building tables in GM Core, across the full d20 range including natural 1s and 20s. Three findings drive every recommendation that follows.

- **Player offence rises steeply.** Attack rolls and spell DCs run roughly +2 over standard math at 1st and 5th level, +6 by 10th, +7 by 15th, settling to +5 at 20th.
- **That bonus crosses the critical threshold.** Because Pathfinder triggers a critical hit at ten over the target number, the increase converts ordinary hits into critical hits rather than misses into hits. Against Extreme AC, critical hits rise from 5 percent to between 25 and 30 percent.
- **Player defence keeps pace.** Enhanced Defense reduces an Extreme attacker from 80 percent hits and 30 percent criticals against a well-built character to 60 percent and 10 percent by 15th level.

*The practical consequence is narrow and specific. Your creatures need substantially more Hit Points, modestly better Armour Class and saves, and the same attack bonus they already had.*

**The Variant Rules Compound This**

Dual Class, Free Archetype, and Ancestry Paragon do not change the figures above, which are driven by the Expertise Tracks and the attribute caps. What they change is how often those figures apply and how many answers a character has.

- **Proficiency floors rise.** Dual Class means every character takes the better weapon and spell progression of two classes. Characters already Expert in weapons run a rank ahead of the Weapon Expertise minimum until 13th level, so what was a Fighter or Gunslinger quirk becomes common.
- **Options roughly double.** Twice the class feats, an archetype feat every even level, and an ancestry feat every odd level. Expect characters to have a prepared answer to most situations you construct.
- **Action economy improves indirectly.** More feats means more ways to spend a turn efficiently, which compounds with Talents such as Borrowed Time and Warmage.

*Plan encounters on the assumption that the party has a tool for everything. Difficulty should come from the shape of the fight rather than from the party lacking an option.*

## Raise Hit Points First

This is the single most effective adjustment, and for most encounters it is the only one you need. Damage per round against Extreme-difficulty opposition runs between 1.9 and 3.3 times standard, rising to 5.4 times for a character with Unbroken Tempo. A fight built to the ordinary budget ends in a third of the expected rounds.

| Level | Damage vs standard | With Unbroken Tempo | Suggested HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 1.9x | 2.2x | x2 |
| 5th | 2.4x | 2.9x | x2.5 |
| 10th | 3.0x | 3.9x | x3 |
| 15th | 3.3x | 5.4x | x3.5 |
| 20th | 2.6x | 4.3x | x3 |

*Where the party includes a character with Unbroken Tempo, Warmage, or Bonded Soul, use the upper figure. Each of those roughly doubles a character's effective actions on top of the track bonuses.*

## Raise Defences Modestly

Moderate and High arrays stop challenging these characters after the early levels. Extreme arrays remain meaningful throughout, and are the sensible default from 5th level onward.

- Use the Extreme band for Armour Class and saving throws from 5th level.
- Add 2 to those values from 15th, where the offensive gap is widest.
- Below 5th level, the High band is sufficient.

*Against spell DCs the picture is starker. A creature with a Moderate save fails against these casters 75 to 80 percent of the time, and critically fails between 25 and 35 percent, against a standard 5 percent. Creatures you expect to resist magic need the Extreme save band, not merely a good one.*

## Leave Attack and Damage Alone

Enhanced Defense has already closed most of the gap on the receiving end. A creature that hits harder or more often is not compensating for anything; it is simply killing player characters.

| Level | Without Enhanced Defense | With it |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 80% / 30% crit | 75% / 25% |
| 5th | 80% / 30% | 70% / 20% |
| 10th | 80% / 30% | 65% / 15% |
| 15th | 80% / 30% | 60% / 10% |
| 20th | 80% / 30% | 60% / 10% |

*An Extreme-attack creature of the party's level, unmodified, remains a genuine threat at every level. That is the correct baseline.*

## Do Not Raise Monster Level

It is the obvious correction and it is the wrong one. Restoring standard hit rates requires creatures four to five levels above the party, and their attack bonus scales with them.

| Party Level | Level Needed | That Creature vs a PC |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 2nd | 85% hits, 35% criticals |
| 5th | 8th | 95% hits, 45% criticals |
| 10th | 14th | 95% hits, 45% criticals |
| 15th | 20th | 95% hits, 50% criticals |
| 20th | 24th | 90% hits, 40% criticals |

*A creature four levels above the party critically hits a well-built character roughly half the time. Raise Hit Points and defences instead, and reserve level adjustment for a single climactic opponent where the lethality is the point.*

## The Low Levels Are the Dangerous Ones

Spellcasting Expertise adds two riders to every creature a spell damages: the proficiency bonus and the caster's spellcasting attribute modifier. Both apply per creature, so an area spell multiplies the pair across every target.

| Level | Rider | Across 6 | Low-HP Creature | Share of Its HP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 8 | 48 | 16 | 50% |
| 5th | 12 | 72 | 59 | 20% |
| 10th | 15 | 90 | 135 | 11% |
| 15th | 18 | 108 | 210 | 9% |
| 20th | 19 | 114 | 285 | 7% |

This runs opposite to intuition. The riders are most devastating at 1st level, where they alone account for half a lesser creature's Hit Points before a single die is rolled, and become a rounding error by the midgame.

- Groups of weak creatures do not function as encounters against an area caster at levels 1 through 4. Use them as spectacle.
- From 5th level the problem resolves itself, and ordinary encounter budgets work again.
- Budget Hit Points per encounter rather than applying one flat multiplier. A single-target martial and an area caster strain entirely different parts of the budget.

## Talents That Remove Your Tools

Most of this ruleset adjusts numbers, and numbers can be answered with numbers. A handful of Talents instead remove a category of encounter design outright. These are worth knowing before they surprise you at the table.

- **Unbound Casting** at 10th and **Unbroken Tempo** at 15th — action denial stops working. Slowed, Stunned, and Paralyzed no longer prevent that character from acting. Damage, unconsciousness, and physically removing them from the fight still do.
- **Dread Presence** at 17th — nothing in the game is immune to the aura, including mindless and fear-immune creatures.
- **Immovable** at 15th — an anti-teleport aura. Creatures that rely on translocation to escape or reposition lose that option near this character.
- **Sovereign Mind** at 10th and **Piercing Gleam** at 15th — Will-based incapacitation, illusions, and most mental effects stop landing.
- **Flicker Step** from 1st — the whole party relocates 100 feet per action through solid obstacles, scaling to a mile. This reshapes maps and journeys more than fights.
- **Haven** and **The Ledger** — attrition, encumbrance, and supply pressure largely stop being levers you can pull.

## What Still Works

Nothing in this ruleset is genuinely uncounterable. Every numerical bonus can be answered by a numerical adjustment on your side of the screen, and where a Talent removes an immunity or an outcome, it does so narrowly and the creature still rolls.

- **Hit Points and action economy.** More creatures, better positioned, acting more often, is the most reliable pressure available to you. It scales smoothly and it never feels arbitrary.
- **Terrain and objectives.** A fight the party cannot win by damage alone is a fight their damage multiplier does not solve. Hostages, timers, collapsing ground, and objectives that must be reached rather than killed all remain fully effective.
- **The talents themselves.** Every character has exactly three, and each covers a narrow slice. A party that has spent its picks on offence is genuinely fragile; one that has spent them on defence is genuinely slow. Design against the gaps rather than the strengths.
- **Their own weaknesses.** Deathless names a damage type that switches it off. Living Shadow needs dim light. Trail Breaker leaves allies falling when a fight starts mid-flight. These are written into the Talents deliberately, and using them is fair play rather than spite.

*Favour Extreme arrays, apply templates freely, multiply Hit Points without apology, and leave attack bonuses where the Bestiary put them. That combination holds up at every level this ruleset covers.*
